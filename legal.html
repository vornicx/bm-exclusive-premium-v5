Site information

This website is a presentation redesign of Mfinity’s public-facing car-rental experience. Vehicle names, contact details, published rates and business information are presented from Mfinity’s publicly available material and should be confirmed directly with Mfinity before a rental.

Reservations and payments

The website does not itself confirm a rental or process payment. Availability requests continue through direct contact with Mfinity. Final vehicle availability, pricing, deposits, rental conditions and handover details are confirmed by the business.

Cookies and local storage

The presentation does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. Essential browser storage may be used by the interface or external services when needed for normal operation. External destinations such as WhatsApp apply their own policies.

Accuracy

Availability and commercial information can change. Users should rely on Mfinity’s direct confirmation for a transaction.
