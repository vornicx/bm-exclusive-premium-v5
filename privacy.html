Privacy information

This presentation website does not process payments or maintain customer accounts. Availability enquiries are prepared in the interface and continue through WhatsApp, where the user chooses whether to send the message.

Information entered in the enquiry form

Dates, vehicle preference, name, phone number and notes are used only to compose the WhatsApp enquiry on the user’s device. This prototype does not intentionally store those form values in its own database.

External services

The site may load public media from Mfinity and interface assets from trusted content delivery services. When you choose to continue to WhatsApp or another external destination, that service’s own privacy terms apply.
